1. The 200-Unit Protected Slab Strategy

In Pakistan, domestic electricity tariffs set by NEPRA follow steep progressive slabs:

  • Protected Category (≤ 200 kWh/month): Subsidized lifeline rate (~Rs 14 to Rs 20 per unit).
  • The Tariff Cliff (≥ 201 kWh/month): Crossing into 201 units doubles or triples your rate (~Rs 45 to Rs 60+ per unit).
  • The 6-Month Penalty Rule: Crossing 200 units revokes your Protected status for 6 consecutive billing months!
The Multi-Meter Advantage: By legally dividing home electrical loads across 4 independent single-phase meters (e.g. Main House, Garage, Studio, Basement), families keep each connection under 200 units. Meter Record serves as your mission control to ensure no connection breaches the threshold.

2. Initial Setup & Meter Configuration

  1. Set Electricity Provider (DISCO): Open Settings → Select LESCO, K-Electric, IESCO, FESCO, GEPCO, MEPCO, etc.
  2. Usage Thresholds: Default is set to Warn at 150 kWh and Ceiling at 200 kWh.
  3. Configure Meters: Go to the Meters tab and assign names, 14-digit reference numbers, distinct color tags, and the monthly billing cycle anchor day.

3. Logging Daily/Weekly Meter Readings

Locate the digital LCD screen on your meter. Wait until it cycles to the Active Cumulative Import Energy (kWh) screen. Note down the whole number before the decimal point.

  • Tap the central Electric Lime + button.
  • Select the meter you are logging for.
  • Enter the reading using the large keypad (or tap microphone for Voice Entry).
  • Tap Save Reading.
Tip: Logging readings once every 7 to 10 days gives the prediction engine enough datapoints to accurately project your cycle-end finish.

5. Consolidated Household Monthly Bills

Access the Household Bills overview by tapping the summary card on Home. Here, you see:

  • Total Combined Household Expense (e.g. Rs 10,640 across all 4 meters).
  • Total Household kWh and your true effective cost per unit (e.g. Rs 20.38/kWh).
  • Paid vs Unpaid Progress Bar showing real-time balance.
  • Individual meter cards with 1-tap paid toggles and reference numbers.

6. 30-Second Batch Bill Entry Guide

When your monthly utility bills arrive, tap + Record Month Bills in the top right corner:

  1. Verify the Bill Date and Due Date.
  2. Fill in the units and rupee amounts for all 4 meters in one form.
  3. Watch the live Household Total auto-calculate at the bottom.
  4. Tap Save All Bills. All 4 bills are recorded in seconds!

Batch Entry Modal with Live Household Sums

7. 1-Tap Bill Payment & Copying Reference Numbers

To avoid late payment surcharges:

  • Tap the clipboard icon next to any meter's reference number to copy its 14 digits.
  • Open your banking app (Meezan Mobile, Nayapay, SadaPay, Easypaisa, or JazzCash).
  • Paste directly into the Bill Payment screen.
  • Return to Meter Record and tap Mark Paid with an optional note (e.g. "Nayapay").

8. Trends, Historical Comparisons & Heatmaps

Navigate to the Trends tab to view cycle-by-cycle consumption graphs, meter load shares, and heatmaps showing high-usage spikes across weeks.

9. Bilingual Urdu Support (اردو)

10. Home Screen Widgets

Long-press your phone's home screen → Tap Widgets → Select Meter Record → Drag to place. View per-meter consumption without opening the app.

11. Data Privacy, Backups & PDF Exports

Meter Record is 100% offline. No third-party trackers or ad networks.

  • PDF Report: Generate clean, print-ready reports in Settings.
  • CSV Export: Export your raw reading history to Excel or Google Sheets.
  • JSON Backup: Create full encrypted backups to transfer data between phones.

12. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Can I change a meter's reference number?
Yes, tap any meter in the Meters tab to edit its 14-digit reference number, name, or color tag anytime.
Q: What if I entered the wrong reading?
Select the meter, go to its Readings list, tap the incorrect entry, and choose Edit or Delete.
Q: How do I handle month-to-month DISCO reading delays?
When logging a bill or reading, select the actual date it was read. Meter Record dynamically computes daily burn rates based on real elapsed days.
