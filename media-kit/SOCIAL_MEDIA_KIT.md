## 💼 LinkedIn Launch Post

**Headline**: Engineering a solution for Pakistan's multi-meter household electricity reality ⚡📱

With recent tariff revisions in Pakistan, the gap between "Protected" ($\le 200$ kWh/month) and "Unprotected" domestic consumers has become massive. Crossing just 1 unit over 200 not only doubles your rate but strips your household of protected status for the next six months.

To manage this, many Pakistani homeowners operate multiple independent single-phase meters across their property (e.g. 4 meters: Main House, Garage, Studio, Basement). However, managing 4 disparate billing cycles, paper slips, 14-digit reference numbers, and due dates is fraught with friction.

To solve this, I designed and developed **Meter Record (میٹر ریکارڈ)** — a modern, offline-first Android application built with Jetpack Compose, Material 3, and Room SQLite.

Key architectural & UX highlights:
🔹 **Multi-Meter Dashboard**: Live cycle tracking & threshold watchdog (Warn at 150, Ceiling at 200).
🔹 **Consolidated Household Bills**: Aggregates total monthly expense and true effective Rs/kWh across all meters.
🔹 **Late-Fee Prevention**: Proactive due-date banners and 1-tap reference number clipboard integration for fast banking app payments.
🔹 **30-Second Batch Entry**: Fast modal with live state derivations to log entire household bills in seconds.
🔹 **Bilingual RTL Parity**: Native Urdu typography alongside English.
🔹 **Privacy-First**: No ads, no cloud requirement, 100% local database with JSON backups and CSV/PDF reports.

Check out the full media kit and screenshots in the repository.
