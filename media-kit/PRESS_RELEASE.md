### Pakistani Indie Developer Launches "Meter Record": A Smart Utility App Helping Multi-Meter Households Stay Under the 200-Unit Electricity Slab and Consolidate Bills

**OKARA, PAKISTAN** — Today marks the official announcement of **Meter Record (میٹر ریکارڈ)**, an offline-first Android application designed specifically to address the economic and operational complexities of residential electricity consumption in Pakistan.

Under prevailing NEPRA tariffs, domestic consumers maintaining 200 units (kWh) or fewer per month receive heavily subsidized "Protected" status. However, breaching this 200-unit ceiling on a single bill can double or triple the consumer's tariff bracket and disqualify the household from subsidized rates for six consecutive months.

To legally distribute electrical loads, thousands of Pakistani homes operate multiple independent single-phase meters (typically between two and four meters on a shared board). While this strategy keeps individual connections under the 200-unit cap, it creates substantial administrative friction: tracking disparate billing cycles, monitoring physical meters across walls or floors, and tracking multiple 14-digit reference numbers and due dates to avoid late-payment surcharges.

**Meter Record** solves these challenges through an elegant, private, and high-performance native Android experience:

* **Real-Time Protected Slab Watchdog**: Projects month-end consumption pace and triggers proactive alerts when any meter approaches 150 or 175 kWh, allowing families to balance loads before meters breach the 200-unit ceiling.
* **Consolidated Household Monthly Bills Dashboard**: Provides a unified financial view of all four meters side-by-side, calculating combined household electricity expenditure, total units billed, and real effective cost per unit.
* **Rapid 30-Second Batch Entry**: Homeowners can record all four monthly paper bills in a single dialog with live auto-summing calculations.
* **Late-Fee Prevention & 1-Tap Clipboard**: Displays prominent home screen warning banners before bills fall due, and provides one-tap copying of 14-digit consumer reference numbers directly into Pakistani digital banking apps (Meezan Mobile, Nayapay, SadaPay, Easypaisa).
* **Native Urdu & English Parity**: Built with comprehensive Right-to-Left (RTL) typography and phrasing tailored to Pakistani users.
* **Zero Cloud Lock-in & 100% Privacy**: Operates fully offline using a local encrypted SQLite/Room database, with zero advertisements, zero background trackers, and complete one-tap JSON, CSV, and PDF export capabilities.

"Residential electricity management in Pakistan is no longer passive; it is an active financial budgeting discipline," said Hassan, lead developer of Meter Record. "Our goal was to build a tool that feels like an executive financial cockpit—fast, completely offline, beautiful, and tailored specifically to how Pakistani multi-meter homes actually operate."

### Availability
Meter Record is available for Android running Android 8.0 and above. The app is completely free, ad-free, and contains no in-app purchases.
