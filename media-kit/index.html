The Story Behind the App

In Pakistan, residential electricity tariffs have steep non-linear slabs. Staying at or below 200 units/month grants "Protected" status with subsidized lifeline rates. Crossing just 1 unit beyond 200 doubles your base rate and strips protected status for 6 consecutive months.

To manage this legally, many homes run multiple independent single-phase meters (e.g. 4 meters: Main House, Garage, Studio, Basement). Meter Record consolidates these scattered connections into one beautiful, private, and actionable cockpit.

🚀 Key Capabilities

🏠 Multi-Meter Command Center

Monitor 4+ independent physical connections side-by-side with color-coded tier cards (Safe Green, Caution Amber, Warning Orange, Danger Red).

🛡️ ≤200 kWh Protected Slab Watchdog

Real-time pace projections and threshold warnings before any connection breaches 150 or 175 units, protecting your low tariff bracket.

🧾 Consolidated Household Bills

Aggregates total combined monthly household expenditure, true effective Rs/kWh, and paid vs unpaid ratios across all 4 meters.

⚡ 30-Second Fast Batch Entry

Log all 4 monthly bills in under 30 seconds in a single modal with live dynamic household sum calculations.

⚠️ Due-Date Warning & 1-Tap Copy

Urgent Home warning banners prevent late-payment surcharges; 1-tap copies the clean 14-digit reference number for Meezan, Nayapay, or Easypaisa.

🇵🇰 Bilingual RTL Parity (Urdu & English)

First-class Right-to-Left Urdu experience with localized typography, natural phrasing, and high-contrast Dark and Light themes.

📰 Official Press Release

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE: Pakistani Indie Developer Launches "Meter Record"

Full press release text (available in media_kit/PRESS_RELEASE.md):

OKARA, PAKISTAN — Today marks the official announcement of Meter Record (میٹر ریکارڈ), an offline-first Android application designed specifically to address the economic and operational complexities of residential electricity consumption in Pakistan. Under prevailing NEPRA tariffs, domestic consumers maintaining 200 units (kWh) or fewer per month receive heavily subsidized "Protected" status. Breaching this 200-unit ceiling on a single bill can double or triple the consumer's tariff bracket and disqualify the household from subsidized rates for six consecutive months. To legally distribute electrical loads, thousands of Pakistani homes operate multiple independent single-phase meters. Meter Record provides real-time protected slab watchdogs, a consolidated household monthly bills dashboard, 30-second batch bill entry, and 1-tap clipboard reference numbers for mobile banking apps.
