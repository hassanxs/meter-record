## 📝 Full Description (English)

Tired of juggling 4 separate electricity bills, surprise slab penalties, and late payment fees?

**Meter Record (میٹر ریکارڈ)** is the smart, offline-first household electricity tracker tailored specifically for Pakistani homeowners and multi-meter residences (LESCO, K-Electric, IESCO, FESCO, GEPCO, MEPCO & more).

Whether you run a single meter or 4 independent connections (Main House, Garage, Studio, Basement) on a shared board, Meter Record gives you total visibility over your electricity consumption, billing cycles, and payment due dates.

---

### 🌟 KEY FEATURES:

⚡ **MULTI-METER COMMAND CENTER**
• Track up to 4+ physical, independent electricity meters side-by-side.
• Monitor billing cycle days elapsed, daily consumption pace, and projected end-of-cycle units.
• Instant color-coded usage tiers: Safe Green (≤150), Caution Amber (150–175), Warning Orange (175–199), and Danger Red (≥200).

🛡️ **PROTECT YOUR ≤200 kWh LIFELINE SLAB**
• Don't let a single AC or heavy appliance push you into costly unprotected slabs!
• Receive smart mid-cycle alerts before crossing 150 and 175 kWh thresholds.
• Protect your subsidized lifeline tariff status and save tens of thousands of rupees annually.

🧾 **CONSOLIDATED HOUSEHOLD BILLS & DUE-DATE TRACKER**
• See total combined household electricity expense (e.g., Rs 10,640 across 4 meters) in one clean hero dashboard.
• Know your true effective cost per unit (Rs/kWh) across all connections.
• **Avoid Late Surcharges**: Urgent warning banners remind you before bills fall due.
• **1-Tap Batch Bill Entry**: Enter all 4 meter bills in under 30 seconds with live household sum calculations.
• **1-Tap Reference Number Copy**: Copy clean 14-digit consumer reference numbers directly to your clipboard for fast pasting into Meezan, Nayapay, SadaPay, or Easypaisa banking apps.
• **Payment Status**: Mark bills as paid with 1-tap and track payment method notes (e.g., Meezan App, Nayapay).

🇵🇰 **FULL URDU & ENGLISH SUPPORT (اردو)**
• Complete, beautiful Right-to-Left (RTL) Urdu translation and layout.
• Switch effortlessly between English and Urdu with zero compromise.

🔒 **100% PRIVATE, OFFLINE & AD-FREE**
• No accounts, no logins, no cloud tracking, and ZERO advertisements.
• All data remains safely stored on your device in a high-performance local SQLite database.
• Export CSV spreadsheets, share PDF reports, or back up your entire history with one tap.
• Optional biometric app lock (fingerprint / PIN).

📱 **HOME SCREEN WIDGETS**
• Keep an eye on per-meter usage and protected status directly from your Android home screen with modern Glance widgets.
